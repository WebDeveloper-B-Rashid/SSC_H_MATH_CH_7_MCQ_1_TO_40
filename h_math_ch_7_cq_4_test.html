[Answer Any Two Questions. Each question carries 10 marks (2+4+4)]
1. \(\frac{1}{2x+5} + \frac{1}{(2x+5)^2} + \frac{1}{(2x+5)^3} + \dots\)
(a) If \(x = 1\), determine the series and find the common ratio of the obtained series.
(b) If \(x = -1\), find the 15th term and the sum of the first 30 terms of the series.
(c) Under what condition on \(x\) will the series have a sum to infinity? Find that sum.
2. (i) \(\frac{1}{2x-7} + \frac{1}{(2x-7)^2} + \frac{1}{(2x-7)^3} + \dots\)
   (ii) \(8 + 88 + 888 + \dots\)
(a) Find the \(n\)-th term of the series: \(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{3}{8} + \frac{1}{4} + \dots\)
(b) Determine the condition on \(x\) for the series (i) to have an infinite sum.
(c) Find the sum of the first 15 terms of the series (ii).
3. The \(n\)-th term of a geometric series is \(U_n = \frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{(2x)^n}\), \(n \in \mathbb{N}\)
(a) Determine the series.
(b) If \(x = 1\), find the 6th term and the sum of the first 10 terms of the series.
(c) What condition must be applied to \(x\) in the series obtained in 'a' for it to have a sum to infinity? Find that sum.
4. In an infinite geometric series, the first term is greater than the third term by \(\frac{63}{100}\), and the third term is greater than the fifth term by \(\frac{252}{2500}\).
(a) Forming equations based on the stimulus, let the first term be \(a\) and the common ratio be \(r\).
(b) Determine the sum to infinity of the series.
(c) If the partial sum of the first \(n\) terms of the series is \(\frac{15465}{12500}\), find the value of \(n\).
